Brand Identity

BleSaf Logotype Proposals

10 creative directions grounded in 2026 design trends, each with detailed design guidelines and ready-to-use Nano Banana generation prompts.

About This Document

BleSaf (from Arabic Saf = queue/row + French Ble = wheat/harvest) is a virtual queue management SaaS for Tunisian medical clinics. These 10 proposals explore radically different visual directions while respecting the brand's core palette (teal, navy, amber) and bilingual (French + Arabic) requirements.

Each proposal includes a copy-ready Nano Banana prompt — click the Copy button to use it directly.

The Grain Counter

Archival Modernism
Core Concept

A single wheat grain rendered as a counting bead on an abacus wire. Three notches mark counted positions. The metaphor: each patient is a precious grain being counted and moved forward. Evokes Tunisian souks where merchants count goods with care, married to the precision of digital tracking. Mid-century geometry meets contemporary precision — "New Nostalgia."

Visual Description
Typography: "Ble" in a medium-weight geometric sans (Futura/Gilroy style) with rounded terminals. "Saf" in semi-bold at a slightly lower baseline. Accent on "e" replaced by a tiny amber wheat kernel.
Icon/Mark: A horizontal line (the wire) passes through a rounded teardrop (the grain/bead). Three tiny notches on the left (patients counted), open space on the right (queue ahead). 1:1 square container.
Negative Space: The gap between grain and notches reads as "movement along a track" — forward progress.
Scaling: At 16px favicon: circle on a line. At billboard: wheat grain texture and wire notches become visible details.
Color Palette
#0F7B6C Teal (mark)
#1E3A5F Navy (wire + text)
#F59E0B Amber (accent)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Mark left, wordmark right, wire extends to card edge
  • App icon: Grain-on-wire centered on white/cream
  • Signage: Grain enlarged, wire becomes underline
  • Animation: Grain slides left-to-right, pausing at notches
  • Dark mode: Wire + text white, grain stays amber, bg #1C4A3B
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" below Latin, sharing the wire as connector. Arabic 18% larger
Nano Banana Prompt
Professional logotype design for "BléSaf" brand, a medical queue management SaaS. The logo features a single stylized wheat grain shaped like a rounded teardrop sitting on a thin horizontal line that acts as an abacus wire. Three small notch marks appear on the left side of the wire, suggesting counted positions. The wheat grain has a single subtle interior crease line. The word "Blé" is set in a clean geometric sans-serif typeface similar to Futura Medium with rounded terminals, and "Saf" in the same typeface at semi-bold weight, positioned slightly lower creating a staggered baseline. The accent on the é is replaced by a tiny amber wheat kernel shape. Color palette: deep teal (#0F7B6C) for the grain mark, navy blue (#1E3A5F) for the wire and wordmark text, warm amber (#F59E0B) for the accent kernel. Style reference: mid-century modern counting instruments reimagined with contemporary precision, similar to Pentagram's work for the Science Museum or Massimo Vignelli's systematic approach. Clean vector rendering, mathematically precise geometry, no gradients, no shadows, no textures. White background, centered composition, generous whitespace. Avoid: stethoscopes, crosses, medical symbols, hearts, generic tech icons, stock illustration style. The design should feel like a premium SaaS product identity, not a healthcare clip art.

The Woven Script

Variable Expressionism
Core Concept

The word "BléSaf" rendered as a single continuous stroke that weaves between Latin and Arabic calligraphic DNA. Letterforms interlock like threads on a loom — "Ble" (wheat = harvest = woven textiles) meets "Saf" (row = warp threads). The stroke thickens and thins as it moves, borrowing from Arabic Naskh pressure modulation while maintaining Latin legibility. Typography IS the identity.

Visual Description
Letterforms: A single continuous line from "B" to "f." Stroke varies 2px to 12px, echoing nib pressure. The "e" flows into a flourish mirroring Arabic ص (Sad). The "S" borrows its upper curve from Arabic س (Sin). No letter is disconnected.
Icon: None. Pure wordmark. The letter connections form the visual identity.
Negative Space: Counters of "B" and bowl of "a" create two "eyes" — open circles suggesting watchfulness, attentiveness.
Scaling: Small sizes: stroke variation reduced for uniformity. Large: calligraphic modulation fully expressive.
Color Palette
#1C4A3B Deep Forest Teal (single color)
#F6F5F0 Cream (reversed)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Wordmark centered, no icon, ample whitespace
  • App icon: Monogram "BS" extracted from the continuous stroke
  • Signage: Large-scale wordmark, dramatic stroke variation
  • Animation: Stroke draws itself B-to-f like a calligrapher writing in real time
  • Dark mode: Cream (#F6F5F0) on dark teal (#1C4A3B)
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" in matching custom Arabic style below — sibling expression of same calligraphic system
Nano Banana Prompt
Custom hand-lettered logotype wordmark for "BléSaf" rendered as a single continuous calligraphic stroke. The line begins at the letter B and flows unbroken through l, é, S, a, f, varying in thickness from very thin (2px equivalent) to thick (12px equivalent), mimicking Arabic Naskh calligraphy nib pressure. The stroke modulation is smooth and organic, not jagged. The letter "é" flows with a flourish that subtly echoes the Arabic letter Sad (ص). The "S" in Saf borrows its upper curve from the Arabic letter Sin (س). Letters are connected but each remains individually legible. No separate icon or symbol -- the wordmark IS the identity. Single color: deep forest teal (#1C4A3B). Typography style reference: the calligraphic precision of Luca Barcellona meets the structural clarity of Jessica Hische's lettering work. The counters of "B" and the bowl of "a" should be prominent open shapes. Clean vector quality, smooth bezier curves, no rough edges, no ink splatter, no grunge texture. White background, horizontally centered, generous whitespace above and below. Avoid: separate Arabic text, icons, medical symbols, generic script fonts, brush script casualness. This should feel intentional, structured, and premium -- controlled expressiveness, not spontaneity.

The Tessellation

Authenticity Seal + Negative Space
Core Concept

Inspired by Tunisian zellige tilework, the logo is a geometric tile pattern that contains the letters "B" and "S" interlocked in the negative space. The tiles reference the waiting room floor of every Tunisian clinic — transforming a symbol of tedious waiting into beautiful order. The queue (saf) IS the pattern: organized, repeating, harmonious. The tile can tessellate infinitely.

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: A square tile rotated 45° (diamond shape) containing interlocking triangles and parallelograms. Negative space between shapes forms "B" on the left, "S" (or Arabic ص) on the right. Two tones + negative space only.
Typography: "BléSaf" in IBM Plex Sans Medium beneath the tile, generous letter-spacing (+60). "Digital Health" in IBM Plex Sans Light, small caps below.
Negative Space: The B and S are revealed by empty spaces — an "aha" discovery moment that creates sticky cognitive bonds.
Scaling: 16px: simple geometric diamond with light/dark split. Large: hidden B/S and zellige complexity emerge.
Color Palette
#0F7B6C Teal (tile shapes)
#1E3A5F Navy (tile shapes)
#FFFFFF White (negative space letters)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Single tile watermark across card, wordmark in lower third
  • App icon: Diamond tile on teal bg, B/S negative space centered
  • Signage: Strip of repeating tiles as border, wordmark below
  • Animation: Tile rotates, internal pattern shifts between B/S reveal and pure geometry
  • Dark mode: Tile in light teal + cream on #1C4A3B
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Medium, same tracking. Tile is script-agnostic
Nano Banana Prompt
Geometric logotype for "BléSaf" featuring a diamond-shaped tile mark inspired by Tunisian zellige mosaic tilework. The tile is a square rotated 45 degrees containing an interlocking geometric pattern of triangles, parallelograms, and angular shapes arranged in perfect symmetry. The negative space between the geometric shapes subtly reveals the letters "B" on the left half and "S" on the right half of the diamond. The tile uses exactly two colors: teal (#0F7B6C) and navy (#1E3A5F), with white negative space forming the hidden letters. Below the diamond tile mark, the word "BléSaf" is set in IBM Plex Sans Medium with generous letter-spacing, in navy (#1E3A5F). Below that, "Digital Health" in IBM Plex Sans Light small caps. Style reference: the geometric precision of Islamic geometric art combined with the branding clarity of Wolff Olins or Landor. The tile pattern should be mathematically precise with perfect angles and symmetry, as if it could tessellate infinitely. Clean flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects. White background, centered composition. Avoid: ornate decoration, curves, organic shapes, floral motifs, medical symbols, rounded forms. Everything is angular, precise, architectural.

The Horizon Line

Strategic Whitespace
Core Concept

Ultra-minimal. A single horizontal line with a gap at the golden ratio point. From the right edge of the gap, a short vertical element rises — a wheat stalk, a person standing up, a moment of being called. The line IS the queue. The interruption IS "your turn." 95% empty space. The meaning lives in what is NOT there. Influenced by Japanese "Ma" (negative space as design element).

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: Single horizontal line, 2px weight. Interrupted at 61.8% (golden ratio) by a gap. A short vertical element rises from the gap's right edge, exactly 1.618x the line height. Like a wheat stalk or person standing.
Typography: "BléSaf" in Helvetica Neue Thin or Inter Thin above the line, with the gap aligned to fall between "Ble" and "Saf."
Negative Space: The gap IS the logo. Everything is about absence — the moment the queue opens.
Scaling: 16px: horizontal line with a dot. Billboard: thinness against vast whitespace creates dramatic presence.
Color Palette
#1E3A5F Navy (monochrome)
#0D9488 Teal (optional vertical accent)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Line extends edge-to-edge, gap at golden ratio. Details above the line
  • App icon: Line + gap centered in white square. Remarkably simple
  • Signage: Full-width line, gap illuminated (backlit slot). At night: only the gap glows
  • Animation: Gap moves left-to-right, brief vertical rise at end — "called"
  • Dark mode: White line on #1C4A3B, gap becomes a void within light
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" below the line — bilingual reflection with line as mirror axis
Nano Banana Prompt
Ultra-minimalist logotype for "BléSaf" consisting of a single thin horizontal line with a small gap at the golden ratio point (approximately 62% from the left). From the right edge of the gap, a short vertical element rises upward, exactly 1.618 times the height of the line thickness. The line is 2px weight equivalent, perfectly straight. Above the line, the word "BléSaf" is set in a very thin geometric sans-serif typeface similar to Helvetica Neue Ultralight or Inter Thin, with the space between "Blé" and "Saf" aligned directly above the gap in the line. The entire composition is monochrome navy blue (#1E3A5F) on pure white background. Style reference: the radical minimalism of Kenya Hara's MUJI branding, John Pawson's architectural philosophy, or the reductive clarity of Peter Saville's work. The design should feel like a haiku -- every element is essential, nothing can be removed. No icons, no symbols, no decorative elements, no gradients, no shadows. Pure geometry, pure line, pure space. Centered composition with massive whitespace surrounding the mark. Avoid: complexity, decoration, medical imagery, circles, curves, color, thickness, heaviness. This logo is about absence, not presence.

The Amber Field

Controlled Imperfection
Core Concept

Warm, organic, human. The "e" accent sprouts into a small three-kernel wheat ear — not generic, but recognizable. The wordmark feels hand-written with warmth. This is not cold technology; it's a tool made by humans who understand waiting frustration. The wheat connects to Tunisian identity (wheat = staple crop = sustenance = essential). Like the best bakery signs — controlled imperfection.

Visual Description
Typography: Custom hand-lettered wordmark. "Ble" in rounded, bouncy script with uneven baselines. "Saf" transitions to bolder, more structured strokes. The "f" descender curls back as a subtle underline.
Icon: The accent on "e" transforms into a 3-kernel wheat head: three ovals in a fan with two tiny awns (whiskers). Grows directly from the letter — not a separate icon.
Negative Space: The curl of the "f" creates an enclosed area — a subtle "safe space" or "waiting area" shape.
Scaling: 16px: wheat ear simplifies to a single amber dot (accent). Large: hand-drawn character becomes a charming focal point.
Color Palette
#1C4A3B Warm Dark Teal (letterforms)
#F59E0B Amber (wheat ear)
#F6F5F0 Cream (background)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Wordmark on warm cream cardstock, amber is the only color. Artisanal feel
  • App icon: The "e" with wheat ear accent, extracted as standalone glyph on cream circle
  • Signage: Full wordmark, large, on wood or cream bg. Welcoming, not clinical
  • Animation: Wheat ear grows — first one kernel, then two, then three. Gentle blooming
  • Dark mode: Cream letterforms, amber wheat, bg #1C4A3B
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" in matching warm, imperfect style. Wheat kernel above "ب" echoes the accent
Nano Banana Prompt
Warm hand-lettered logotype for "BléSaf" with organic, slightly imperfect letterforms that feel human and approachable. The word "Blé" is written in a rounded, bouncy custom script with slightly uneven baselines and gentle curves. "Saf" continues in the same hand but transitions to slightly bolder, more structured strokes. The accent mark on "é" transforms into a small three-kernel wheat head: three tiny oval kernels arranged in a fan pattern with two delicate awns (wheat whiskers) extending upward. The wheat ear grows organically from the letter. The descender of the final "f" curls back underneath the word, creating a subtle decorative underline. Color: warm dark teal (#1C4A3B) for all letterforms, warm amber (#F59E0B) for the wheat ear accent only. Background: warm cream (#F6F5F0). Style reference: the warmth of Rifle Paper Co's hand lettering combined with the structural intelligence of Erik Marinovich's custom type work for artisan brands. The imperfection should be controlled and intentional, not sloppy -- like a master baker's handwriting. No digital perfection, no geometric precision, but clear legibility. No icons separate from the type. Avoid: cold colors, sharp angles, generic font usage, medical symbols, tech aesthetic. This should feel like bread and sunshine, not software.

The Signal Tower

Z-Axis Layering
Core Concept

A bold geometric mark: a vertical rounded rectangle (tower/minaret silhouette — referencing Tunisian urban architecture) emits three concentric quarter-arcs from its top-right corner. The tower is the clinic. The signal arcs are the real-time notifications reaching patients. Unapologetically technical and architectural — a serious infrastructure tool. Borrows from telecommunications iconography but roots it in North African architectural forms.

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: Vertical rounded rectangle (1:2.5 ratio, ~15% corner radius) with 3 quarter-arc lines from upper-right corner at 45°. Arcs at 100%, 60%, 30% opacity. A horizontal slot near the top = simplified window/door.
Typography: "SAF" in Bebas Neue (from brand system), "Ble" in a medium geometric sans. Wide letter-spacing. Horizontal or stacked lockup.
Negative Space: The slot in the tower creates "opening" — an invitation. Space between arcs suggests expanding reach.
Scaling: 16px: rectangle with a single arc. Billboard: three arcs, slot, and opacity variation create sophisticated layering.
Color Palette
#1E3A5F Navy (tower)
#0D9488 Teal (signal arcs)
#F59E0B Amber (window slot)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Mark top-left, arcs extend toward center where contact info sits
  • App icon: Tower + arcs on navy bg, tower in white, arcs in teal
  • Signage: Tower cut from metal/acrylic, arcs painted or illuminated
  • Animation: Arcs pulse outward from tower in sequence — broadcast/ping
  • Dark mode: Tower white, arcs light teal (#3D8A6E), bg #1C4A3B
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold, left of mark (RTL reading direction)
Nano Banana Prompt
Bold geometric logotype for "BléSaf" featuring a symbol-heavy icon mark. The mark consists of a vertical rounded rectangle (tower/minaret shape, aspect ratio 1:2.5, with ~15% corner radius) from whose upper-right corner three concentric quarter-arc lines radiate outward at 45 degrees. The arcs decrease in opacity: innermost at 100%, middle at 60%, outermost at 30%. The tower has a small horizontal slot or gap near its top third, like a simplified window. To the right of the mark, "BléSaf" is set with "Blé" in a geometric sans-serif medium weight and "SAF" in a condensed bold display face like Bebas Neue, both with wide letter-spacing. Colors: tower in navy blue (#1E3A5F), signal arcs in teal (#0D9488), the tower slot accent in amber (#F59E0B), "Blé" text in teal, "SAF" text in navy. Style reference: the architectural iconography of Snohetta's branding work, combined with the signal/broadcast aesthetic of telecoms marks like the old AT&T globe or Cisco's bridge lines. Hard edges, precise geometry, no organic curves, no hand-drawn elements. Clean vector, flat design, no gradients on the tower (arcs may use opacity variation). White background, centered. Avoid: literal buildings, antenna dishes, WiFi symbols, medical crosses, soft shapes. This should feel like infrastructure -- solid, reliable, permanent.

The Breath Between

Atmospheric Gradients
Core Concept

A pure typographic identity built on "nafas" (breath/space between). The wordmark uses outlined letterforms where only the enclosed counter-spaces (inside B, e, a) are filled with a teal gradient — the letters glow from within. Open letters (l, S, f) remain pure outlines. The rhythm of full/empty mirrors the queue: some positions occupied (glowing), some open (outlined). What matters is not the container but the space inside — the patient, the person waiting.

Visual Description
Typography: "BléSaf" in a humanist sans-serif (Avenir/Nunito style). Strokes are outlines only (no fill), 2.5px weight, in navy. Enclosed counters of B, e, a filled with gradient (teal bottom to light teal top).
Icon: None. The gradient-filled counters ARE the identity. Three glowing shapes within outlined letters.
Negative Space: Unfilled letters (l, S, f) create a rhythm of full/empty — occupied/available positions.
Scaling: 16px: outlined wordmark with 3 colored dots. Large: gradient and outline/fill interplay fully appreciable.
Color Palette
#1E3A5F Navy (outlines)
#0D9488 Teal (gradient bottom)
#5EEAD4 Light Teal (gradient top)
#F6F5F0 Warm White (background)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Wordmark centered on warm white. Three glowing counters = only color
  • App icon: Letter "B" outlined, counter filled with teal gradient. Glowing monogram
  • Signage: Backlit — counter-spaces are only illuminated areas. Three soft teal glows
  • Animation: Gradient rises inside each counter sequentially (B, e, a) — breathing rhythm
  • Dark mode: Cream outlines, deeper gradient fills, bg #1C4A3B. Glow intensifies
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" outlined, counters of ب and ص filled with same gradient
Nano Banana Prompt
Typographic logotype for "BléSaf" using outlined letter forms with gradient-filled counter spaces. All six letters are rendered in a humanist sans-serif similar to Nunito or Avenir at medium weight, but the strokes are OUTLINES ONLY (no solid fill) in navy blue (#1E3A5F) at 2.5px stroke weight. The enclosed counter spaces INSIDE the letters B, é, and a are filled with a soft vertical gradient transitioning from teal (#0D9488) at the bottom to light teal (#5EEAD4) at the top, creating a gentle internal glow. The open letters l, S, and f remain pure outlines with no fill. The result is a rhythm of glowing and empty letters. The spacing between letters is generous and even. Background: warm off-white (#F6F5F0). Style reference: the luminous quality of James Turrell's light installations translated into typography, combined with the typographic sophistication of Commercial Type's work. The gradient should be soft and seamless, not banded. The outlines should be perfectly uniform in weight. No separate icon, no additional elements. Clean vector rendering. Centered horizontal composition with ample whitespace. Avoid: solid filled letters, drop shadows, 3D effects, glow effects around the letters (the glow is INSIDE the counters only), medical symbols, decorative flourishes. Elegant, restrained, quietly beautiful.

The Kufic Gate

Arabic-First + Archival Modernism
Core Concept

An homage to Square Kufic calligraphy — the ancient geometric script used in mosques and manuscripts. Arabic letters ب (Ba) and ص (Sad, initials of "Ble Saf" in Arabic) rendered on an 8x8 grid where every curve becomes right angles. The two letters interlock, sharing strokes, forming both a readable monogram AND an abstract geometric mark. Arabic leads — Latin is secondary. Honors the fact that "Saf" (صف) is an Arabic word for an Arabic-speaking market.

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: Perfect square on 8x8 grid. Arabic ب and ص in Square Kufic — every stroke is a rectangle filling grid cells. Letters share vertical and horizontal strokes. A diamond dot below (dot of ب) is the only non-rectangular element.
Typography: "BléSaf" in IBM Plex Sans Semi-Bold right of or below mark. "بلي صف" in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold as equal-weight companion.
Negative Space: Unfilled grid cells create small squares = windows in a facade, empty chairs in a waiting room.
Scaling: 16px: dense square with recognizable pattern. Large: Kufic letterforms legible, grid structure revealed.
Color Palette
#0F7B6C Deep Teal (mark)
#F59E0B Amber (diamond dot)
#1E3A5F Navy (wordmarks)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Kufic mark debossed or foil-stamped on cream card. Both scripts on reverse
  • App icon: Kufic square fills entire icon space, teal on white. Culturally confident
  • Signage: Metal grille: filled cells solid, empty cells open = mashrabiya lattice effect
  • Animation: Grid fills cell by cell in a wave, revealing letters progressively
  • Dark mode: Light teal (#3D8A6E) on dark, amber dot persists
  • Arabic: Arabic IS primary. "بلي صف" appears first + larger. "BléSaf" second + smaller
Nano Banana Prompt
Square Kufic calligraphy logotype for "BléSaf" brand. The primary mark is a perfect square containing the Arabic letters Ba (ب) and Sad (ص) rendered in Square Kufic geometric calligraphy style where every curve is converted to right angles and every stroke is a rectangle fitting within an 8x8 grid. The two letters interlock and share strokes where possible, creating a dense, abstract geometric pattern within the square. A single small diamond shape below represents the dot of the letter Ba. Below or beside the square mark, "BléSaf" in IBM Plex Sans Semi-Bold and "بلي صف" in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold are set as a bilingual lockup, with the Arabic text slightly larger (18% larger) than the Latin. Color: the Kufic mark in deep teal (#0F7B6C), the diamond dot in amber (#F59E0B), wordmarks in navy (#1E3A5F). Style reference: historical Islamic Square Kufic calligraphy from the Great Mosque of Isfahan or Alhambra tile inscriptions, reinterpreted with the precision of a modern branding system similar to Tarek Atrissi's Arabic identity work or Rana Abou Rjeily's experimental Arabic typography. The grid should be mathematically precise. All angles are 90 degrees. No curves whatsoever in the Kufic mark. Clean flat vector, no textures, no aging effects. White background. Avoid: decorative Arabic calligraphy, flowing scripts, floral motifs, generic geometric patterns without letter content, orientalist aesthetics. This should feel simultaneously ancient and digital.

The Living Counter

Generative Identity
Core Concept

The logo is a number. Specifically, a stylized "#" (hash/number sign) representing queue position — the most important number in a patient's waiting experience. The hash bars are offset (shifted 15%) creating forward-leaning energy. The rounded terminals suggest crossing human figures. In digital contexts, a live number appears: "#3". The identity is never the same twice, reflecting the real-time nature of the queue.

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: Hash (#) from four rounded rectangles — verticals shifted right 15%, horizontals shifted up 15%. Dynamic, forward-leaning. Center creates a diamond-shaped opening = "center of order."
Typography: "BléSaf" in DM Sans Bold or similar, slightly condensed. Below the mark. Hash bars match wordmark stroke weight for unity.
Dynamic element: In digital, a number appears right of hash: "#3" in amber. Changes in real time. Static version uses "#" alone.
Scaling: 16px: hash immediately recognizable. Large: offset arrangement and rounded terminals give character beyond generic symbol.
Color Palette
#0D9488 Teal (hash mark)
#F59E0B Amber (live number)
#1E3A5F Navy (wordmark)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Hash printed oversized, extending off edges. System always running
  • App icon: "#" in teal on white. Badge shows actual queue count
  • Signage: Hash + live digital number of patients waiting. Sign IS a dashboard
  • Animation: Four bars assemble from corners, cross center, then number counts up
  • Dark mode: Light teal (#5EEAD4) hash, amber number, white text
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" below. Hash is universal. Arabic-Indic numerals in AR context
Nano Banana Prompt
Modern logotype for "BléSaf" featuring a stylized hash/number sign (#) as the primary mark. The hash is constructed from four rounded rectangles: two vertical and two horizontal, with rounded terminal ends. The vertical bars are shifted approximately 15% to the right and the horizontal bars shifted approximately 15% upward from center, creating a dynamic, forward-leaning composition rather than a perfectly centered grid. The rounded ends of the bars subtly suggest human figures. To the right of the hash mark, the number "3" is displayed in a bold weight, representing a queue position. Below the combined mark, "BléSaf" is set in a bold slightly condensed sans-serif like DM Sans Bold. Colors: hash mark in teal (#0D9488), the number "3" in amber (#F59E0B), wordmark text in navy (#1E3A5F). The stroke weight of the hash bars should match the stroke weight of the wordmark letters for visual unity. Style reference: the systematic identity work of Sagmeister & Walsh or the data-driven branding of MIT Media Lab's generative identity. The hash should feel kinetic and alive, not static. Clean vector, no gradients, rounded corners on all bar terminals. White background, centered composition. Avoid: literal hashtag social media associations, pound signs, musical sharp symbols, thin lines, generic number fonts. This should feel like a living dashboard element, not a social media tag.

The First Light

Tactile Materiality
Core Concept

A crescent — not the religious symbol, but the first sliver of dawn light entering a room. In Tunisia, clinic waiting rooms open early morning. This captures the moment the day begins, the queue opens, the first patient is called. The crescent is abstract: a thick arc that could be a smile, a bowl of wheat, or the letter "C" for "clinique." An amber dot at its threshold = the first patient, the first grain, the first light. It feels like a physical object you could hold — brass, ceramic, warm.

Visual Description
Icon/Mark: Thick crescent arc (~270°) with 90° opening facing right. Consistent width, not tapered = solidity. A single circle (dot) sits at the threshold, half inside/half outside. Inner edge offset slightly upward = thicker bottom, thinner top. Bowl-like.
Typography: "BléSaf" in IBM Plex Sans Semi-Bold to the right. Horizontal lockup.
Negative Space: Crescent interior = the "waiting space." Threshold dot = moment of release.
Scaling: 16px: bold crescent + contrasting dot. Large: subtle gradient and bowl asymmetry become tactile.
Color Palette
#0F7B6C Deep Teal (arc)
#1C4A3B Dark Teal (gradient base)
#F59E0B Amber (threshold dot)
#1E3A5F Navy (wordmark)
Brand Environment
  • Card: Mark left, wordmark right, on thick uncoated cream cardstock
  • App icon: Crescent + dot on white. Simple, identifiable in app grid
  • Signage: Crescent in brushed brass or matte teal metal, amber dot as LED element
  • Animation: Amber dot orbits inside crescent (like marble in bowl), exits at opening
  • Dark mode: Lighter teal arc (#2D6B55), amber persists, bg #1C4A3B
  • Arabic: "بلي صف" below Latin. Crescent can stay facing right for brand consistency
Nano Banana Prompt
Logotype for "BléSaf" featuring a tactile crescent arc mark with a threshold dot. The mark is a thick crescent arc covering approximately 270 degrees of a circle, with the 90-degree opening facing right (3 o'clock position). The arc has consistent stroke width, not tapered, giving it a sense of physical solidity and weight like brushed brass or matte ceramic. The outer edge is a perfect circle; the inner edge is a circle offset slightly upward, making the bottom of the arc slightly thicker than the top, like a vessel or bowl. At the opening point of the crescent, a single circle (dot) sits precisely at the threshold -- half inside the arc's interior space, half outside, as if emerging from the opening. To the right of the mark, "BléSaf" is set in IBM Plex Sans Semi-Bold. Colors: crescent arc in deep teal (#0F7B6C) with an optional very subtle linear gradient from darker (#1C4A3B) at the bottom to the teal at the top suggesting light on a physical surface. Threshold dot in warm amber (#F59E0B). Wordmark in navy (#1E3A5F). Background: warm cream (#F6F5F0). Style reference: the material quality of Minale Tattersfield's identity work, the warmth of Ragged Edge's branding, the physical presence of Wolff Olins' Tate identity. The mark should feel like an object you could hold -- a pendant, a ceramic piece, a brass fitting. Clean vector but with visual weight and presence. Avoid: thin lines, religious crescent moon associations, star and crescent combinations, Islamic symbols, flat lifeless rendering. This is about dawn light, physical warmth, and the threshold moment.
