Issues to Fix Before France Launch

Blocking and non-blocking issues specific to AuSuivant

Critical

CheckInPage Phone Validation

CheckInPage.tsx imports formatTunisianPhone — French patients can't enter +33 numbers. Must use brand-aware phone formatting.

Critical

Stripe Production Testing

Stripe gateway is implemented but never tested with real or test-mode payments on Railway. Webhook secret must be configured in production env vars.

Warning

Email Template Colors

Email templates use hardcoded emerald (#059669) instead of AuSuivant's forest green (#1B6B4A). Cosmetic but affects brand consistency.

Warning

Admin Panel Uses Legacy Design

BleSaf admin was redesigned with new Financial/Engagement/Clinics pages. AuSuivant still uses older tab-based layout.

Warning

Terms & Privacy Pages

Legal pages reference BleSaf/Tunisia. Need France-specific legal content for AuSuivant SAS / Paris jurisdiction.

Warning

France i18n Coverage

fr-FR.json (15KB) overrides only ~150 keys. Base fr.json (66KB) may contain Tunisia-specific references that leak through.
