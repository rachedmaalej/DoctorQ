A · Inline Banner Recommended
Slides down from below the header. White card with teal left accent border, icon box, and a countdown progress bar. Mirrors the app's settings-item pattern (icon box + text).
  • Most consistent with the existing card/row design language
  • Doesn't overlap queue cards or bottom navigation
  • Progress bar gives clear auto-dismiss feedback
  • Dismiss button for immediate close
  • Takes up vertical space (pushes queue down visually)
  • May feel less "urgent" than a floating element
B · Floating Pill
Dark pill with teal circle icon, floating above the bottom nav. Pulse ring animation draws attention. Compact, minimal footprint. Similar to the patient-status progress toast but redesigned with app tokens.
  • Minimal — doesn't obscure any content
  • The dark bg + teal accent is high contrast and attention-grabbing
  • Pulse ring adds urgency without being jarring
  • Works well across all toast types (call, moveUp, emergency)
  • Dark element on a light page can feel disconnected
  • No explicit dismiss — relies on auto-hide only
C · Card Peek
Slides up from above the bottom nav as a full-width card. Shows patient initials avatar (matching the header avatar pattern), name, and a teal action icon. Progress bar at the bottom.
  • Feels like part of the queue — same card radius and border style
  • Avatar initials reinforce which patient was called
  • Most informational — clear hierarchy of label → name
  • Progress bar for countdown visibility
  • Overlaps the last queue card briefly
  • Larger footprint than pill — more distracting during rapid calls
