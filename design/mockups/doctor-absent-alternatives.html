Patient Status Page: Doctor Absent State

When the doctor hasn't started consultations yet, the patient status page currently shows an empty notification. Below are 5 alternative designs for communicating this state clearly while keeping the patient informed and calm. Each mockup shows position #5 in the queue with the doctor not yet arrived.

A
Muted Waiting Room

Replace the entire hero section with a calm "waiting for doctor" state. The page mood shifts to a warm gray palette. The time estimate is hidden (since it can't be accurate without the doctor). Position is preserved in a minimal card.

Strengths Honest: no misleading estimate. Very clear visual distinction from normal state. Feels calm, not alarming.
Trade-offs Loses the time estimate entirely. Bigger engineering change (new hero variant). May feel "empty" to patients.

B
Prominent Amber Card

A rich amber card appears between the header and hero section, clearly stating the doctor hasn't arrived. The normal queue view remains visible below, preserving the familiar layout. The time estimate stays but the card contextualizes it as "once consultations begin."

Strengths Prominent without replacing existing layout. Patient keeps their time estimate for reference. Easy to implement (enhanced banner).
Trade-offs Time estimate may confuse (it starts "when doctor arrives"). Takes vertical space.

C
Full-page Calm State

The entire page becomes a dedicated "waiting for doctor" view. A clock icon with gentle animation occupies the center. Position is confirmed, a start time is shown, and a notification CTA lets patients be alerted when consultations begin. When the doctor arrives, the page smoothly transitions to the normal queue view.

Strengths Clearest possible communication. No ambiguity. Natural place for "notify me when it starts" CTA. Elegant transition opportunity.
Trade-offs Largest engineering change (new full page state). Hides queue info. May feel like "nothing is happening."

D
Inline Hero Swap

The hero section swaps its eyebrow, time, and ring to reflect the "paused" state. The eyebrow reads "CONSULTATIONS EN PAUSE", the time shows a projected start rather than wait time, and the ring shows the patient's position with a dashed border instead of a solid progress arc.

Strengths Keeps the familiar layout structure. Position + projected start are both visible. Moderate engineering effort.
Trade-offs "~9h30" in the hero could be confused with a wait time. The ring loses its progress meaning. Mixed metaphor (start time vs wait time).

E
Enhanced Banner + Dimmed Hero Recommended

The smallest, most surgical change. A richer amber banner (with icon box, title, and body) replaces the current empty one-liner. The hero time estimate is dimmed to 35% opacity and gains a "PAUSE" pill badge, signaling that the estimate is suspended. The ring is also dimmed. When the doctor arrives, opacity restores smoothly (0.4s transition) and the banner slides out — zero layout shift.

Strengths Minimal code change. No layout shift. Preserves position + estimate for reference. Clear "pause" signal. Smooth transition when doctor arrives (just un-dim + hide banner).
Trade-offs Dimmed estimate might still confuse some patients. Less dramatic than a full-page takeover.
