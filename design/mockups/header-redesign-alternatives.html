Alt 1 — Inline Push-Down

Response content expands directly below the drawer, pushing stats + queue downward. No overlay.
No overlayOrganic flowScrollable
+ Content feels part of the page — no jarring modal or overlay
+ User can still see the drawer buttons to switch tool
- Long content (Parametres) pushes queue far down
- May feel less "contained" for quick-glance tools like QR

Alt 4 — Right Slide Panel

Each tool slides in a full-screen panel from the right. Back arrow returns to the dashboard.
Full-screenSpaciousNavigation feel
+ Maximum space for every tool — QR is large, settings are comfortable
+ Feels like a sub-page — natural navigation with back arrow
- Full context switch — user loses sight of the queue entirely
- Overkill for a quick QR glance or WhatsApp send

Alt 5 — Popover from Chip

QR, WhatsApp, Duree open as floating popovers with a caret pointing to the source chip. Parametres opens a full side panel (too much content for a popover).
ContextualLightweightConnected
+ Popovers feel directly connected to their trigger button
+ Lightweight — doesn't feel like a full-screen takeover
- Parametres needs a different pattern (side panel) — inconsistency
- Popover positioning can be tricky on small screens
