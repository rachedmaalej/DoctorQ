3 — Slide-Down Gradient Panel

Medium complexity Low perf. cost

  • Dramatic slide-down from top — feels like a system-level alert
  • Staggered content animation (icon → title → message → button)
  • Feathered gradient edge fades into the page below
  • Bold blue gradient creates strong visual contrast
  • Best for urgent announcements ("Doctor leaving", "Clinic closing")

4 — Split-Screen

Medium complexity Low perf. cost

  • Page layout transforms — announcement takes top 60%, status compresses below
  • Patient can still see their position at all times (no information loss)
  • Curved divider between zones adds a polished feel
  • Least disruptive — ideal for persistent, long-duration announcements
  • Smooth expand/collapse animation when dismissed
