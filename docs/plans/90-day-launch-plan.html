Marketing Strategy (4P)

BleSaf — Tunisia

P Product

Digital queue: QR check-in, real-time tracking, multi-doctor, Arabic+French. No app install. Web-based for both doctors and patients.

P Price

65 TND/month or 650 TND/year (2 months free). 30-day free trial. "Less than a coffee per day." ROI: prevents ~150 patient losses/year = 4,500-7,500 TND saved.

P Place

Physical + digital hybrid. blesaf.tn. WhatsApp doctor groups, physical card delivery, Facebook/Instagram ads. Target: Tunis clinic-dense areas (Les Berges du Lac, Ennasr, Menzah).

P Promotion

3-beat teaser campaign (Mystery → Reveal → Founding Clinic). WhatsApp-first follow-up. Facebook retargeting ads (Week 8+). Founding clinic quarterly dinner. Testimonial-driven word-of-mouth.

AuSuivant — France

P Product

Same core product. RGPD-compliant (name anonymization). Receptionist-focused UX. Complementary to Doctolib (queue management ≠ appointment booking).

P Price

49 EUR/month or 490 EUR/year (2 months free). 30-day free trial. Positioned as affordable vs Doctolib (100-130 EUR/month for appointments alone).

P Place

Digital-only. filosoin.fr. LinkedIn, medical secretary Facebook groups, cold email to multi-doctor practices, Google Ads ("gestion file attente cabinet medical").

P Promotion

LinkedIn content (2-3 posts/week). Cold email (200 clinics in Paris/Lyon/Marseille). Google Ads (5-10 EUR/day). Free webinar: "5 ways to improve your waiting room."
