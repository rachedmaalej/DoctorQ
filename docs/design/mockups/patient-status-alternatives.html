1. Current Design Faithful

Polished evolution of the existing BleSaf design

Preserves all current design elements — the ticket card, journey visualization, fun facts, and glass-morphism cards — while adding the smart wait time estimate prominently. Patients who already know the app won't be disoriented.

Key idea: Don't reinvent; refine. Add the wait estimate as a first-class citizen alongside the existing ticket card.

2. Anxiety-Relief Engine

Strategy document faithful — every recommendation implemented

The wait time dominates at 72px. Progress dots show movement. The consultation progress bar reveals what's happening behind closed doors. Contextual tips replace fun facts with actionable guidance.

Key idea: Answer "How much longer?" instantly. Show movement. Reduce anxiety through transparency, not distraction.

3. Journey Tracker

Uber/delivery-app inspired vertical timeline

Borrowed from food delivery and rideshare tracking UX. A vertical timeline stepper shows exactly where the patient is in their journey. The active stage expands to reveal details. Feels modern and familiar to anyone who's tracked a delivery.

Key idea: Leverage patterns people already understand from everyday apps. Make the queue feel like a journey with clear milestones.

4. Zen Waiting Room

Minimalist, nature-inspired anxiety reduction

Psychology-backed approach: soft, nature-inspired colors (sage, sand, sky) with a slow-cycling gradient background. A single large circle contains the wait time. A breathing exercise animation provides a calming focus. Everything unnecessary is stripped away.

Key idea: The waiting room should feel like a calm space, not an information dashboard. Less is more when anxiety is high.

5. Smart Dashboard

Data-rich transparency for informed patients

An information-dense card layout that gives patients maximum visibility into the queue. Shows their wait time and position as the hero, backed by stat cards (average wait, arrival time, completed consultations), a queue visualization bar, and a live activity feed.

Key idea: Some patients want to know everything. Give them data, context, and full queue transparency — reduce anxiety through information, not simplicity.
