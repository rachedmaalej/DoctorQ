Design Comparison

Design Best For Label Clarity
1. Icon + Subtitle Balance of clarity & compactness ★★★★☆
2. Horizontal Maximum label space & mobile ★★★★★
3. Left Border Professional & scannable ★★★★☆

Common fixes across all designs:

  • Greeting says "Bonjour Skander" (no "Dr. Dr." double prefix)
  • Card 2 clearly labeled: "Durée consultation" + "Moyenne par patient"
  • Card 3 clearly labeled: "Temps d'attente" + "Moyenne par patient"
  • Wait time trends use inverted colors (green = decrease, red = increase)
