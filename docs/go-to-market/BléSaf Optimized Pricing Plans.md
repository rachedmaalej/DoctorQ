## 1. Executive Summary

This document presents a new three-tier pricing structure for BléSaf, designed to maximize adoption, drive revenue growth, and increase customer lifetime value. The plans are strategically crafted based on an analysis of your complete feature set, competitor pricing models (Qminder, Waitwhile), and established SaaS packaging principles.

The recommended structure is:

*   **Essentials:** A low-cost entry point focused on core queue management for solo practitioners.
*   **Pro:** The primary growth engine, bundling SMS/WhatsApp, multi-doctor support, and analytics to justify a clear upsell.
*   **Premium:** A high-value tier for sophisticated clinics, unlocking the full "Waiting Room Experience Platform" and advanced features like appointment booking and custom branding.

This model replaces the current single-price structure, creating a clear value ladder that caters to different segments of the Tunisian clinic market.

## 4. Strategic Rationale

*   **Essentials (29 TND):** This plan is priced aggressively to win the market. It includes everything a solo doctor needs to replace their paper system and see the immediate value of BléSaf. It intentionally excludes SMS and multi-doctor support, creating natural barriers that encourage upgrades as a clinic grows.

*   **Pro (59 TND):** This is your core revenue driver. The jump from Essentials is justified by three powerful value propositions:
    1.  **Automated Communication:** The inclusion of a generous monthly SMS/WhatsApp credit bundle is the single biggest driver. This is a high-cost, high-value feature.
    2.  **Multi-Doctor Support:** Unlocks the platform for the majority of clinics that have more than one practitioner.
    3.  **Business Insights:** The daily recap and historical analytics page move BléSaf from a simple utility to a business intelligence tool.

*   **Premium (99 TND):** This tier captures the highest-value customers. The key drivers are:
    1.  **The Full Waiting Room Experience:** Unlocking the split-screen TV turns the waiting room into a marketing and communication channel, a significant upgrade from the basic queue display in Pro.
    2.  **Full Appointment Booking:** This moves BléSaf into the territory of a full practice management solution, competing with more expensive products.
    3.  **Complete Customization:** White-labeling and custom branding are high-value features for established clinics and polyclinics.
